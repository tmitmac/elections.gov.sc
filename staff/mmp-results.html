Sainte-Laguë Calculation

Method used

Eligible parties receive seats according to their share of the nationwide party vote using Sainte-Laguë divisors.

Votes ÷ 1, Votes ÷ 3, Votes ÷ 5, Votes ÷ 7, Votes ÷ 9, ...

The 26 highest quotients receive the 26 initial parliamentary seats. Electorate seats are then compared with each party's proportional entitlement to determine its required list seats and any overhang.
